# Frame

This file states the problem in one sentence and lists what the fields that study reports call its parts.

## The problem in one sentence

How should a student's report be ordered and written so that its readers can find its answer, judge it and reuse it? Those readers are several: a grader, a jury, a host company, a colleague. The report must also meet what the school or the standard requires.

## What the field calls it

| Name | Field | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| **Genre**, **genre family** | applied linguistics, English for Academic Purposes (EAP) | a recurring type of assignment with a purpose and stages; 13 families in Nesi 2012 |
| **Assessed student writing** | corpus linguistics | texts written for a grade, as in the BAWE corpus |
| **Thesis structure** (simple, complex, topic-based, compilation) | genre analysis | the chapter plans of research theses (Paltridge 2002) |
| **Move** | genre analysis | a stretch of text with one rhetorical job; see Swales 1990, linked from the academic papers review |
| **Thesis examination**, **examiner judgement** | higher-education research | how examiners decide (Mullins 2002, Kiley 2004) |
| **Scientific and technical report**, **technical report** | information science, standards | a report on research or on a technical task, with prescribed components (NISO 2010) |
| **Executive summary** | management, standards | a non-technical front summary for decision makers (NISO 2010) |
| **Report components** | information science | title page, summary, abstract, introduction and the rest, as parts a reader uses (Pinelli 1982) |
| **Headings, signalling** | educational psychology | labels that show topic structure (Lorch 1996) |

## What practitioners and French schools call it

| Name | Where | Field's name above |
|---|---|---|
| **rapport de stage** | schools, universities | internship report |
| **rapport de projet** | courses | project report (no standard term found) |
| **mémoire** (de master) | universities | master's thesis |
| **mémoire d'ingénieur**, **rapport de fin d'études**, **PFE** (projet de fin d'études) | engineering schools; the internship report itself is called a mémoire (EOST 2026) | final internship report |
| **grille d'évaluation** | all of them | grading rubric |
| **soutenance** | all of them | oral defence |
| **résumé**, **mots-clés**, **problématique** | French reports | abstract, keywords, the research question with its justification |
| **écrits universitaires**, **littéracies universitaires** | French language-education research | academic writing as a field |
| **rédaction scientifique** | French guides | scientific writing |
| **maître de stage**, **tuteur** | internships | host-company supervisor, school supervisor |

Practitioners say "mémoire" for two different things: a research thesis and an engineer's internship report.
No French term for the genre family of a course project report was found; BAWE has none either.
