# Shapes

A **shape** is the fixed skeleton of one kind of document: every document of that kind has the same parts in the same
order. These are the shapes particular to academic writing. The general ones (note, folder README, report, commit
message) and the reasons for shapes are in [reader-ergonomics](https://github.com/Tewf/reader-ergonomics/tree/v1.1.1/shapes).

## Contents

| Shape | For |
|---|---|
| [academic-report.md](academic-report.md) | a course project report, an internship report, a master's thesis or a technical report: one skeleton with its kinds marked and every heading in French and English, built around reader-ergonomics' report; with [academic-report.tex](academic-report.tex) |
| [research-paper.md](research-paper.md) | an empirical study for a journal, a conference or a preprint server, with [research-paper.tex](research-paper.tex) |
| [mathematical-article.md](mathematical-article.md) | a proof-centred mathematics article on `amsart`, with [mathematical-article.tex](mathematical-article.tex) |
| [proof.md](proof.md) | one long proof in numbered steps, with [proof.tex](proof.tex) |
