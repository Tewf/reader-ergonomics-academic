# Frame: the problem and its names

This file states the problem in one sentence and lists what research and practice call its parts.

## The method has a name

This review is a **multivocal literature review (MLR)**: a systematic review that adds **grey literature** (sources outside journals and books from academic presses) to the formal literature, after Garousi 2019. Grey sources come in three **tiers** by how much control the outlet exerts:

- Tier 1: books, standards, government and official reports, white papers.
- Tier 2: owner web guides, annual reports, presentations, videos, wiki pages.
- Tier 3: blogs, emails, social posts.

How the pool was built is in [protocol](protocol.md); each source and its score are in [sources](sources.md).

## The problem in one sentence

Decide what goes on the slides and what goes in the speaker's voice so that a listener, who cannot slow down or reread, understands and keeps the main points.

## What is new against a page

| Property | Page | Talk |
|---|---|---|
| Pace | the reader sets it | the speaker sets it (**system-paced**) |
| Reread | possible | not possible |
| Source of words | one channel | slide text and voice at once |
| Speaker | absent | part of the medium |
| Seeing the words | at reading distance | at room distance |

## Names for the same things

- Learning theory: **multimedia learning**, **cognitive theory of multimedia learning (CTML)**, **cognitive load theory (CLT)**.
- Principles with a name each: **modality**, **redundancy**, **segmenting**, **signalling**, **coherence**, **spatial contiguity**, **embodiment**, **voice**, **image**.
- Slide design: **assertion-evidence (AE)**, **sentence headline**, **action title** (consulting), **topic-subtopic** or **phrase headline** slide, **bullet slide**, **slideware**, **presentation graphics**.
- Speaker: **instructor presence**, **social agency theory**, **pedagogical agent**, **gesture**, **enthusiasm**.
- Genre: **conference presentation (CP)**, **scientific conference presentation**, **research talk**, **move analysis**, **multimodal genre**, **Q&A** or **discussion session**.
- Room: **legibility**, **viewing distance**, **display image size**, **element height**, **ICT accessibility**.

## Practitioners' names

- TED: **talk**, **big idea**. Duarte: **glance test**, **sparkline**. Reynolds: **Presentation Zen**.
- Kawasaki: **10/20/30 rule**. Lessig: **Lessig method** (also called **Takahashi method**).
- **PechaKucha** (20 slides, 20 seconds), **Ignite** (20 slides, 15 seconds).
- Amazon: **six-page narrative memo** with a "study hall" start. Consulting: **pyramid principle**.
- Computer science: **great research talk** (Peyton Jones), **good talk** (Alon).
- Conference organisers: **instructions for presenters**, **speaker guide**, **presenter information**.
- Accessibility: **accessible presentation**, **inclusive presentation**.
- Teaching centres: **lecture slide design**, **slide deck**, **handout**.
